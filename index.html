How to play

Every piece moves one square in any direction.

Rock beats Scissors · Scissors beats Paper · Paper beats Rock.

You may only capture a piece you beat. You cannot capture your own pieces.

Win by reaching your goal square. Lose if you have no legal moves.

Draw after 50 moves per side without a capture, or threefold repetition.
